Expense Tracker: Money Manager ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, processed, and shared when you use our mobile application.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

The application allows you to enter and store information for managing your finances. This may include:

  • Income and expense records
  • Categories
  • Notes
  • Transaction dates and details
  • Receipt images or other images you choose to attach

1.2 Google Sign-In

The application provides an optional Google Sign-In feature.

If you choose to sign in with Google, we may receive information made available through Google's authentication service, which may include your name, email address, profile information, and authentication-related information required to sign you in.

Google Sign-In is used to authenticate users and provide features associated with the signed-in account.

We do not use your Google account information for advertising purposes.

1.3 Google Drive Backup and Restore

The application provides an optional Google Drive Backup and Restore feature.

If you choose to use this feature, the application uses Google Drive access to upload, store, and restore backup data created by the application.

The backup may contain your Expense Tracker data, which can include financial records such as income, expenses, categories, notes, dates, receipt information, and other application data required to restore your information.

Google Drive access is used only for the application's Backup and Restore functionality.

The application uses Google's drive.file permission scope where applicable. This scope is limited to files that the application creates or opens through the application, subject to Google's authorization and access controls.

We do not use your Google Drive backup data for advertising, analytics, marketing, or selling personal information.

Google Drive backup is completely optional. Your financial information is primarily stored locally on your device unless you choose to use the backup feature.

Backup data stored in your Google Drive account remains subject to Google's privacy practices and your Google account settings.

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the application and to improve its features, functionality, and user experience.

Crashlytics may collect and process information such as:

  • Technical diagnostic information

Crashlytics information is used to improve application stability and performance.
