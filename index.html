Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker: Money Manager

Developer: Mahant Krupa Technologies

Effective Date: 21 September 2026

Expense Tracker: Money Manager ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is handled when you use the application, including information stored on your device, information used for local import and export, and information processed by third-party services used by the application.

The application is designed to work primarily offline. Your financial records and other core application data are stored locally on your device. Core features such as managing expenses, income, categories, transactions, and viewing your financial records can continue to work without an internet connection.

Internet connectivity may be required for certain third-party services and network-dependent functionality, including advertising, Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, and Firebase Remote Config.

By using the application, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information You Enter Into the App

1.1 Financial and Expense Information

Expense Tracker: Money Manager allows you to create and manage your personal financial records. This may include income, expenses, categories, transaction dates, notes, amounts, and other information that you voluntarily enter into the application.

Your financial records are primarily stored locally on your device using the application's local database.

We do not sell your financial records and we do not intentionally use your financial records for advertising personalization.

1.2 Receipt Images and User Content

The application may allow you to attach receipt images or other user-provided content to your financial records.

Receipt images and other user-created content are primarily stored locally on your device for use within the application.

We do not intentionally upload your receipt images or financial records to our own servers as part of the normal operation of the application.

2. Local Import and Export

The application provides local import and export functionality so that you can create or restore application data using files on your device.

2.1 Export

When you choose to export your application data, the application creates a local export/backup file containing information necessary to restore or use your Expense Tracker data. Depending on the export functionality, this may include income, expenses, categories, transaction details, notes, and other application data.

The exported file is named expense_backup.json and is saved locally on your device in the following folder: Downloads/ExpenseTracker/.

The exported backup file remains on your device and is not automatically uploaded by the application to our servers, Google Drive, or any other cloud storage service.

Export is initiated by you, and the exported file remains under your control on your device. You may delete the exported file at any time using your device's file management functionality.

2.2 Import

When you choose to import data, the application accesses the selected local file and uses its contents to restore or add data to the application.

The application does not use local import functionality to collect unrelated files from your device.

Imported files are processed locally for the purpose of providing the import and restore functionality requested by you.

2.3 Storage Permissions

Depending on the Android version, the application may use Android's storage mechanisms required for local import and export functionality.

  • Android 7.0–8.1 (API 24–28): Read and write external storage permissions may be required for supported local import and export operations.
  • Android 10 (API 29): Write external storage may be used for supported export operations under the application's storage configuration.
  • Android 11 and later: The application uses Android's modern storage model for supported file operations and does not request broad "All files access" permission.

Storage access is used only for application functionality such as importing and exporting Expense Tracker files. We do not use storage access to continuously scan or collect unrelated files from your device.

3. Offline Use and Internet Connectivity

Expense Tracker: Money Manager is designed as an offline-first application. Core financial data and application functionality are primarily available without an internet connection.

An internet connection is not required for normal local management of expenses, income, categories, transactions, and other financial records stored on your device.

Internet connectivity is primarily used for network-dependent services such as advertisements and supporting Firebase services.

When an internet connection is available, the application may connect to Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, and Google AdMob for their respective supported functionality.

If an internet connection is unavailable, advertisements may not load or may be unavailable, and network-dependent Firebase services may be unavailable or delayed. The application's local financial data and core offline functionality can continue to operate normally.

4. Firebase Services

4.1 Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand general application usage and improve the application's features, performance, and user experience.

Firebase Analytics may process information such as application interactions, events, app-instance information, device information, operating system information, application version, and other technical or usage information.

We do not intentionally send the financial records that you enter into the Expense Tracker database to Firebase Analytics as part of normal analytics events.

4.2 Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to identify, diagnose, and fix application crashes and technical problems.

Crashlytics may process crash reports, device information, operating system information, application version information, technical diagnostic information, and relevant error details.

We do not intentionally send your financial records to Crashlytics as part of normal crash reporting.

4.3 Firebase Remote Config

We use Firebase Remote Config to remotely manage selected application settings and configuration when network connectivity is available.

Remote Config may be used for application configuration, including advertising settings, advertisement configuration, advertising frequency, advertisement unit IDs, and other selected application settings.

Advertisement configuration and advertisement unit IDs may be obtained from Firebase Remote Config. If Remote Config cannot be reached, the application may use its configured local fallback settings.

Firebase Remote Config is not used by us to store your financial records, receipt images, or exported files.

5. Advertising and Google AdMob

The application may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Depending on the application's configuration, advertisements may include:

  • Banner advertisements
  • Native advertisements
  • Interstitial advertisements
  • App Open advertisements

Advertising settings, advertisement unit IDs, and advertising frequency may be controlled in part through Firebase Remote Config.

When advertisements are displayed, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process information required to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.

Depending on applicable consent choices, device settings, account settings, and applicable privacy requirements, this may include:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device and application information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location derived from network information
  • Information about advertisements displayed or interacted with
  • Technical and diagnostic information
  • Information used for advertising measurement and fraud prevention

The application does not provide a location-based feature and does not request precise device location permission for its own functionality.

We do not intentionally use your income, expenses, financial records, receipt information, imported files, or exported files to personalize advertisements.

Advertisements generally require network connectivity. If the device does not have an internet connection, advertisements may not load, while the application's core offline functionality can continue to operate.

6. Notifications and Reminders

The application may provide notifications and reminders for features such as expense reminders and other user-selected functionality.

Depending on the feature and Android version, the application may use scheduled alarms, including exact alarms where required and permitted by Android.

Notification permission may be required on supported Android versions. You can manage notification permissions through your Android device settings.

The application may use vibration for applicable notifications or reminders.

Notification and vibration functionality does not by itself collect or transmit your financial information.

7. File Sharing

The application uses Android's FileProvider mechanism when necessary to securely share application-generated files or content with another application after an action initiated by you.

File sharing is limited to the files or content required for the requested functionality. The application does not use FileProvider to provide unrestricted access to your device storage.

8. Data Sharing and Third-Party Services

We do not sell or rent your personal information or financial records.

Certain technical or usage information may be processed by third-party services that support the application's functionality. These services include:

  • Firebase Analytics
  • Firebase Crashlytics
  • Firebase Remote Config
  • Google AdMob

These third-party services may process information according to their own applicable terms, privacy practices, security practices, and legal requirements.

Local financial records, local receipt images, and local import/export files are not intentionally transmitted to these services by the application for advertising or analytics purposes.

We may disclose information when reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, protect application security, prevent fraud or misuse, or protect the rights and safety of users and others.

9. Data Storage and Security

Your financial records and receipt information are primarily stored locally on your device.

Exported files are stored locally on your device in the location used for the export operation.

Imported files are read from the local storage location selected or provided for the import operation.

We do not operate our own server for storing your financial records, receipt images, imported files, or exported files as part of the normal operation of the application.

Information sent to supporting third-party services may be protected using security measures and encryption in transit where provided by those services.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

10. Data Retention and Deletion

Financial records and receipt images stored locally remain on your device until you delete them through available application functionality, clear the application's data, or uninstall the application.

Exported files remain on your device until you delete them using your device's file management functionality or otherwise remove them.

Import files selected by you remain subject to the storage and file management practices of your device. The application does not claim ownership of files you import.

Information processed by Firebase Analytics, Firebase Crashlytics, Firebase Remote Config, Google AdMob, or other supporting services may be retained according to the applicable service's retention practices and legal requirements.

11. App Permissions

The application uses permissions required for its implemented features. Depending on the Android version and enabled features, these may include:

  • INTERNET – Used for Firebase services, advertising, and other network-based application functionality. Internet access is not required for the application's core local financial management features.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – Used to determine network connectivity.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – Used to send application notifications and reminders when notification permission is granted.
  • WAKE_LOCK – May be used to support scheduled or time-sensitive background operations when required.
  • VIBRATE – Used for vibration feedback associated with applicable notifications or reminders.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – Used to restore or reschedule applicable reminders after the device restarts.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM – Used for time-sensitive reminders where exact alarm functionality is required and permitted by Android.
  • ADVERTISING ID (AD_ID) – Used by Google Mobile Ads and related advertising services for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and applicable personalization.
  • WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE – On supported older Android versions, used for local export operations when required by the Android storage model.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE – On supported older Android versions, used for local import operations when required by the Android storage model.

The application does not request precise location permission, phone-call permission, SMS permission, contact permission, camera permission, microphone permission, or broad "All files access" permission for its core functionality.

13. Account and Data Deletion

Expense Tracker: Money Manager does not require users to create an account and does not provide Google Sign-In or another online account creation feature.

Financial records and receipt images stored locally on your device can be deleted through the application's available functionality, by clearing application data, or by uninstalling the application.

Exported files stored on your device can be deleted separately using your device's file management functionality.

Because the application does not maintain an online user account or its own server containing your financial records, there is no online application account that requires account deletion.

Information processed by Google, Firebase, AdMob, or other third-party services is subject to the applicable service's policies and retention practices.
