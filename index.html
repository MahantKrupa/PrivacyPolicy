Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker: Money Manager

Developer: Mahant Krupa Technologies

Effective Date: 12 September 2026

Expense Tracker: Money Manager ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information the application may access, collect, use, store, and share when you use the application.

By using the application, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Handle

1.1 Financial and Expense Information

The application allows you to create and manage your personal financial records. This may include income, expenses, categories, transaction dates, notes, amounts, and other information that you choose to enter.

Your financial records are primarily stored locally on your device. We do not sell your financial records and we do not intentionally use them for advertising personalization.

1.2 Receipt Images and User Content

You may optionally attach receipt images or other content to your transactions.

These images and other user-created content are primarily stored on your device. If you choose to use Google Drive Backup, applicable backup data, including supported receipt images or other stored content, may be included in the backup.

1.3 Google Sign-In

The application may provide optional Google Sign-In functionality. If you choose to sign in, Google may provide information such as your name, email address, profile information, and authentication-related information needed to provide the sign-in functionality.

Google Sign-In information is used to provide authentication and account-related functionality. We do not intentionally use your Google account information for advertising purposes.

1.4 Google Drive Backup and Restore

The application provides an optional Google Drive Backup and Restore feature.

If you choose to use this feature, the application may upload backup data to your Google Drive account and later access that backup to restore your application data.

The backup may contain information such as income, expenses, categories, notes, transaction details, receipt information, and other application data needed to restore your records.

Google Drive access is used only for the application's Backup and Restore functionality. We do not use your Google Drive backup data for advertising, analytics, marketing, or selling personal information.

The application uses the Google Drive file access required for its Backup and Restore functionality. Backup data stored in your Google Drive remains under your Google account and Google Drive settings.

2. Firebase Services

2.1 Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand general application usage and to improve the application's features, performance, and user experience.

Firebase Analytics may process information such as application interactions, application events, installation or app-instance identifiers, device information, operating system information, application version, and other technical or usage information.

We do not intentionally send the financial records that you enter into the Expense Tracker database to Firebase Analytics as part of normal analytics events.

2.2 Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to detect, diagnose, and fix application crashes and technical problems.

Crashlytics may process crash reports, device information, operating system information, application version information, technical diagnostic information, and relevant error details.

We do not intentionally send your financial records to Crashlytics as part of normal crash reporting.

2.3 Firebase Remote Config

We use Firebase Remote Config to manage selected application settings remotely.

Remote Config may be used for application configuration, including advertising settings such as whether ads are enabled, ad configuration, and advertising frequency settings.

Firebase Remote Config is not used to store your financial records.

2.4 Firebase Cloud Messaging

The application may use Firebase Cloud Messaging to support notifications and reminders.

Firebase Cloud Messaging may process technical information and device identifiers required to deliver notifications to your device.

3. Advertising and Google AdMob

The application may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob. Depending on the application configuration, the application may display the following types of advertisements:

  • Banner advertisements
  • Native advertisements
  • Interstitial advertisements
  • App Open advertisements

Advertising settings and advertising frequency may be controlled in part through Firebase Remote Config.

When ads are displayed, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process information required to provide, measure, secure, and improve advertising services.

Depending on applicable settings, consent choices, and device or account settings, this may include:

  • Advertising identifiers
  • Device and application information
  • IP address
  • Approximate location that may be derived from network information
  • Information about ads displayed or interacted with
  • Technical and diagnostic information
  • Information used for advertising measurement and fraud prevention

The application does not provide a location-based feature and does not request precise device location permission for its own functionality.

Advertising personalization, where applicable, may depend on user consent, device settings, Google settings, and applicable privacy requirements.

We do not intentionally use your income, expenses, financial records, receipt information, or other financial data entered into the application to personalize advertisements.

Information handled by the application and its supporting services may be used to:

  • Provide and maintain application functionality
  • Store and manage your financial records

The application may provide notifications and reminders for application features such as expense reminders and other user-selected functionality.

Depending on the feature and Android version, the application may use scheduled alarms, including exact alarms where required and permitted by Android.

Notification permission may be required on supported Android versions. You can manage notification permissions through your device settings.

Your financial records are primarily stored locally on your device.

If you use Google Drive Backup, selected application data may be transmitted to and stored in your Google Drive account.

Information sent to supporting third-party services may be protected using security measures and encryption in transit where provided by those services.

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the application. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Financial records stored locally remain on your device until you delete them, clear the application's data, uninstall the application, or remove them through available application functionality.

Google Drive backup data remains in your Google Drive account until you delete the applicable backup or otherwise remove it from your Google Drive account.

Information processed by Firebase, Google AdMob, Google Sign-In, Google Drive, or other supporting services may be retained according to the applicable service's retention practices and legal requirements.

9. App Permissions

The application uses permissions required for its implemented features. Depending on the Android version and enabled features, these may include:

  • INTERNET – Used for Google Sign-In, Firebase services, Google Drive Backup and Restore, advertising, and other network-based functionality.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – Used to determine network connectivity.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE – Used where required to obtain network or Wi-Fi connectivity information.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – Used to send application notifications and reminders when notification permission is granted.
  • WAKE_LOCK – May be used to support scheduled or time-sensitive background operations when required.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – Used to restore or reschedule applicable reminders after the device restarts.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM – Used for time-sensitive reminders where exact alarm functionality is required and permitted by Android.
  • VIBRATE – Used for vibration feedback associated with applicable notifications or reminders.
  • ADVERTISING ID (AD_ID) – Used by Google Mobile Ads and related advertising services for advertising, measurement, fraud prevention, and applicable personalization.

The application does not request precise location permission, phone-call permission, SMS permission, contact permission, or microphone permission for its functionality.
