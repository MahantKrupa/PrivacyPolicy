Privacy Policy

Expense Tracker: Money Manager

Effective Date: 11 September 2026

Expense Tracker: Money Manager ("we", "our", or "us") values your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored, processed, and shared when you use our mobile application.

By using the application, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

1.1 Information You Provide

You may provide information when using certain features of the application, including profile information such as your name and email address.

You may also enter financial information such as income, expenses, categories, notes, dates, and other information that you choose to store in the application.

1.2 Google Sign-In

The application provides an optional Google Sign-In feature. If you choose to sign in with Google, we may receive information made available by Google, such as your name, email address, profile information, and authentication-related information.

Google Sign-In is used to authenticate your account and provide features associated with your signed-in account.

1.3 Google Drive Backup

The application may provide an optional backup and restore feature using Google Drive. If you choose to use Google Drive backup, the application requests access to Google Drive using Google's drive.file permission scope.

This permission allows the application to access files that it creates or opens through the application, subject to Google's authorization and access controls.

When you choose to back up your application data to Google Drive, selected backup data may be uploaded to your Google Drive account. This may include financial records and other application data required to restore your Expense Tracker information.

Google Drive backup is optional. Your financial information is primarily stored locally on your device unless you choose to use the backup feature.

We do not use your Google Drive backup data for advertising, marketing, or selling personal information.

1.4 Firebase Analytics

We use Firebase Analytics to understand how users interact with the application and to improve its features, functionality, and user experience.

Firebase Analytics may collect information such as app usage information, app instance identifiers, device information, approximate technical information, and other information necessary for analytics.

Analytics information may be processed by Google according to Google's applicable privacy practices.

1.5 Firebase Crashlytics

We use Firebase Crashlytics to identify, diagnose, and fix application crashes and technical problems.

Crashlytics may collect information such as crash reports, device information, application information, operating system information, and other technical information necessary to diagnose and improve application stability.

1.6 Firebase Remote Config

We use Firebase Remote Config to remotely manage selected application settings and features.

Remote Config may be used to control certain advertising-related settings, including whether advertisements are enabled and certain advertising configuration or frequency settings.

Firebase Remote Config does not provide us with access to the financial records that you enter into the application.

1.7 Advertising and Google AdMob

The application may display advertisements provided by Google AdMob, including banner, interstitial, and native advertisements. Advertising functionality may be enabled or disabled through Firebase Remote Config.

When advertisements are displayed, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may collect or process certain information, which may include advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location information, information about ad interactions, and other information required for advertising delivery, measurement, fraud prevention, and advertising personalization, subject to applicable settings, permissions, and user choices.

The use and personalization of advertisements may depend on the user's device settings, applicable privacy requirements, and Google's advertising policies.

7. App Permissions

  • INTERNET – Required for Google Sign-In, Firebase services, Google Drive backup and restore, and advertising when enabled.
  • ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE – Used to determine network connectivity.
  • ACCESS_WIFI_STATE – Used to obtain information about Wi-Fi network connectivity where required by the application or supporting services.
  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS – Used to send notifications and reminders when notification permission is granted.
  • WAKE_LOCK – Used when necessary to support scheduled or time-sensitive operations.
  • RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED – Used to restore or reschedule reminders after the device restarts.
  • SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM – Used for time-sensitive reminders where exact alarm functionality is required and permitted by Android.
  • VIBRATE – Used to provide vibration feedback for applicable notifications or reminders.
  • ADVERTISING ID (AD_ID) – May be used by Google Mobile Ads and related services for advertising, ad measurement, fraud prevention, and personalization, subject to applicable settings and user choices.
